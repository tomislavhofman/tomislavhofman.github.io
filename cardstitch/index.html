FAQ

Questions.

Which card games does it work with?

Card detection is trained mostly on Pokémon cards today, so that's where it works best. Other games may work, but less reliably. Better detection across games (Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece and more) is next on the roadmap.

Does it upload my photos anywhere?

No. Detection, cropping, stitching and text recognition run on your phone. There's no account and no CardStitch server. Images only leave the device when you share them yourself. Details are in the privacy policy.

Where are my files saved?

In your phone's Pictures/CardStitch folder, so they show up in your gallery and file manager. Next to each stitched image, the plain front and back crops are saved too.

Does it work with Cardmarket?

CardStitch makes a regular JPEG with both sides in one picture, which is what single-image listings need. You upload it the way you'd upload any photo. CardStitch doesn't connect to your marketplace account or post anything for you, and it isn't affiliated with Cardmarket.

Does it change how my card looks?

No. It crops and scales both sides evenly, nothing else: no straightening, no filters, no retouching. See honest photos.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 and newer, on a phone with a camera. An iPhone version is coming soon.
