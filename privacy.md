# Privacy

*Last updated: 2 November 2026*

This policy covers the website **hofman.tech** itself. Each product has its own policy, listed below.

## Who is responsible

HOFMAN, obrt za računalno programiranje, Osijek, Croatia ([company details](/legal/)), is the controller.
Contact: [tomislav@hofman.tech](mailto:tomislav@hofman.tech).

## What this site collects

- **No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.** The home page and the blog set no cookies and run no analytics.
- **No third-party requests.** Fonts, scripts and images are served from this site. Nothing is loaded from Google or
  any other outside service.
- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you load a page, GitHub processes your IP
  address and request data to deliver and secure the service; see the
  [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Email.** If you write to us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and keep them as long as
  needed for that and for our legal obligations.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its
processing, and ask for a copy of it. Write to [tomislav@hofman.tech](mailto:tomislav@hofman.tech). You may also
complain to the Croatian data protection authority, [AZOP](https://azop.hr).
